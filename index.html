    Process · Eight weeks, typical

    How a world gets made.

    1. 1BlockoutWk 1–2

      Grey boxes, the camera path and scroll pacing. You walk it before anything is pretty.

    2. 2Art passWk 3–5

      Palette, low-poly modelling, and lighting for two times of day.

    3. 3InteractionWk 6–7

      Pins, hovers, transitions, and the copy placed in the scene.

    4. 4ShipWk 8

      Performance pass on real phones, analytics, launch.
