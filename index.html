Stop 1 · Arrival

Arrive under the white pavilion.

The drive ends in a wide paved courtyard. Ramps on either side lead up past the Mapleash lawn to the reception pavilion and its white tent roof. The main block stands to the right, with a thatched balcony along the upper floor.

  • Paved courtyard parking
  • Reception pavilion
  • Landscaped lawn
  • Thatched balcony

Stop 2 · Pool

An infinity pool facing the valley.

The main pool's far edge looks straight out over forest and tea gardens. A round, shallow pool with steps sits alongside for children, next to a covered play area and a thatched gazebo.

  • Infinity-edge pool
  • Children's pool
  • Kids' play area
  • Thatched gazebo

Stop 3 · Cottages

Cottages with their own veranda.

Each cottage sits on a raised plinth among the trees, with a wooden-railed veranda that glows warm in the evening. Step through the door to see a room.
